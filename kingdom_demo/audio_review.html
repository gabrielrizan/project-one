Kingdom demo — what you actually hear

Open this file in a browser. Every clip below is the exact file in the game.

The day bed already contains crows. Measured: ~42 bird-like events across its 212 seconds, roughly 12 a minute — those are the distant ones you liked. They are part of the recording and repeat with the loop. Everything in the tables below is layered on top of that.

What to tell me

For anything above: keep, quieter, rarer, or remove. The two knobs are level (dB) and how often it fires — both are single constants.
