Audio assets from Freesound.org
===============================

Sourced CC0 or CC-BY only. Never -SA (share-alike) or -NC (non-commercial).

Files were obtained as Freesound's public high-quality OGG previews (192 kbps),
which are served unauthenticated from cdn.freesound.org. The original masters sit
behind an OAuth2 download and were judged not worth the friction: 192k OGG is
already better than the material it replaced, and the difference is inaudible
under a stylised low-poly game.

⚠ THE CC-BY FILES BELOW MUST BE CREDITED IN THE SHIPPED GAME.
   See ATTRIBUTION-REQUIRED.md next to this file — that is the checklist.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Licence:  CC0 1.0 (both sources)
Edited:   crow_a is the source used whole (it already held exactly one call).
          crow_b/c are two calls cut out of Corbeau 1. All tail-trimmed, 8 ms
          fades, peak normalised.
Why:      Third attempt. The first pool held SEQUENCES (one file had nine calls,

          MEASURE THE LENGTH OF THE CALL ITSELF, not just the file.
Used for: the daytime crow burst (ambience.gd::_crow_burst), 1-3 calls weighted
          towards one, spaced 1.15-1.9s so drawn-out calls do not stack.

          ARROW_WOOD_IMPACT_SINGLE_ARCHERY  by JoeDinesSound   (/534956/)
          Fruit Impact 1                    by OwlStorm        (/209012/)
Licence:  CC0 1.0 (all three)
Edited:   Tail-trimmed to ~0.3 s and peak normalised. Nothing else -- these are
          real recorded archery impacts and need no doctoring.

coin pickup — no new files
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
The monarch banking a loose coin (coin.gd::_bank_it) reuses the SAME
coins-in-cloth takes the tally spends out of -- one material for one substance.
It is pitched to 0.74, below the tally's whole rising ramp (0.80 .. 1.22), and
sits at -12 dB, so gold coming in never gets mistaken for gold going out.


tree_fall_1.ogg / tree_fall_2.ogg         [CC0 — no credit needed] (EDITED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Titles:   "A Tree Falling Down.wav" by ecfike       https://freesound.org/s/139952/
          "timber tree falling 2" by matt_beer      https://freesound.org/s/515312/
Licence:  CC0 1.0 (both)
Edited:   ALIGNED TO THE ANIMATION. Each clip's loudest moment was measured and
          the head trimmed so the crash sits exactly 1.09 s in -- which is when
          the trunk hits the grass in tree_resource.gd (0.24 s of lean plus
          FALL_TIME 0.85 s). The sound starts with the topple, so its creak and
          whoosh cover the fall and the crash lands ON the impact.
          ecfike's crash was at 1.35 s, matt_beer's at 3.18 s; both now 1.09 s.
          They were also REBALANCED after sounding "watery", like a tree falling
          into a lake. Measured, they were low/high 0.13 -- almost pure swish,
          no weight. Fix was a spectral tilt (+4 dB under 900 Hz, -9 dB above)
          plus a heavy wood impact mixed in ON the crash frame:
            "Iron-Wood Slam" by Hakren  https://freesound.org/s/372877/  CC0 1.0
          Now 0.46 and 1.46. The lead-in is also ducked to 0.72x the crash --
          boosting the lows otherwise let an earlier crack become the loudest
          moment, which silently broke the alignment.
⚠ If the lean or FALL_TIME ever change, THESE CLIPS MUST BE RE-ALIGNED.
Used for: tree_resource.gd::_topple.


fire_light.ogg                            [CC0 — no credit needed] (EDITED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Title:    "Fireblast med.wav"
Author:   cribbler
Source:   https://freesound.org/s/381855/
Licence:  CC0 1.0
Edited:   High-passed below 150 Hz, long fades in and out, trimmed to 1.9 s.
Chosen:   0.49 s attack -- a swell rather than a hit.
Why:      REPLACED lollosound's torch pass, which read as an explosion. The
          measurement explained it: that clip's low/high energy ratio was 11.57,
          i.e. almost all sub-bass, and a bass-heavy burst IS a boom however slow
          its attack. Stripping the sub took the new one to 3.32 and turned the
          bang into air moving.
Used for: campfire.gd::light. Its own emitter, so the catch rings over the
          crackle loop starting rather than cutting it off.
