# The Last Monarch

Founded by **legacy** and **naughty_x**.

A village builder made in Godot 4.7, with a stylized flat-shaded low-poly 3D look. You
play the monarch, riding through your kingdom and spending gold to set things in motion
while your people do the actual building. Light the campfire, feed the cauldron to draw
in wanderers, and watch your village grow through the day, then hold on through the night.
